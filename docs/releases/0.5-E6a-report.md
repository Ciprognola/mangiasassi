# E6a — balance measurement report (2026-09-28)

Report-only pass: **no change to `index.html`**. Measures how gironi 1–12 play with the E1–E5 abilities
(§10.3), for both characters, so the owner can pick tuning directions before E6b applies anything.

## Method

- New harness: `tools/fbstub/sim_e6.py` (kept in the repo, re-run for E6b). Headless Chromium, dev mode,
  `cancelAnimationFrame(raf)` then `update(1/60)` driven directly from the test script (the same
  "freeze the loop, step by hand" pattern the other `tools/fbstub` tests already use for the maze, and
  that `faStep` uses for Ferma Algidone!).
- **Bot**: a simple BFS pathfinder, identical shape for both characters, no secret abilities. At every tick
  it targets the nearest pellet (or, while powered up, the nearest scared ghost within 4 tiles), and picks
  the neighbouring tile with the smallest BFS distance to that target. Tiles within 2 of a non-scared
  ghost/Super Panino, or of a projectile's next couple of tiles along its path, are avoided when a route
  still exists without them; if avoidance would make every target unreachable, it retries at radius 1, then
  0 (never gives up and idles, but will walk into danger rather than stall forever camped by one guarded
  pocket).
- **Difficulty**: Normale (`medium`) only, both characters, gironi 1–12.
- **Seeds**: 5 per (character × girone), reused identically for the girone's own scheduled ghost stages and
  for the stage-1 ablation (`E1_STAGE_OV=1`, i.e. no abilities at all) — `Math.random` is replaced with a
  small seeded xorshift generator at the start of each run. **240 runs total, wall time 192s** (well inside
  the ~15 min budget), no drop to 3 seeds needed.
- Each girone runs until `G.state==='clear'` or 180 simulated seconds (timeout). `G.lives` is forced to 999
  so a death never ends the run early (`finishRun()` never fires) — deaths are counted by the harness
  instead, via a wrapped `killPlayer()` that classifies the cause (ghost / projectile / Super Panino) by
  replaying the same distance checks the two real call sites use, at the exact moment it fires.
- Instrumentation wraps a small set of already-existing functions (`MPROJ.spawn`, `GREASE.add`,
  `PANINO.startIntro`/`eatSplit`, `smashFx`, `killPlayer`) to tally events — nothing in `index.html` changes;
  these are page-side monkey-patches applied by the harness script only, for the duration of one run.

### Two real bugs in the bot, caught and fixed before any numbers were trusted
Both are documented in code comments in `sim_e6.py`, since the script is kept for E6b:
1. **Decision timing**: recomputing the bot's next direction every external tick still wasn't enough —
   when a tick's own movement crosses a tile boundary with time left over, the game's own `step()` loop
   immediately re-reads `P.next` for the new tile *within the same tick*, before the harness gets another
   chance to update it. The bot ping-ponged forever between two tiles, one tile short of a pellet it had
   already correctly targeted. Fixed by anticipating the crossing (deciding for the tile about to be
   entered whenever one is imminent), the same way a human taps the next direction just before a junction.
2. **Target flip-flop**: re-picking "the nearest pellet" fresh every tick let two similarly-distant pellets
   swap which one looked nearer as the player crossed between two adjacent tiles, walking back and forth
   between them forever. Fixed by committing to a target until it's actually eaten.
   Reproduced and fixed with ghosts disabled entirely, confirming it had nothing to do with avoidance.

## Caveats (the bot's own limits)

- **Noise at 5 seeds**: girone 1 forces stage 1 in both the "scheduled" and "stage-1 ablation" runs — the
  ghosts are identical either way — yet the delta table still shows +1.2 deaths / +14.5s between them,
  purely from comparing two different batches of 5 seeds. Treat any `|Δ|` in the ablation delta tables
  within about ±1.5 deaths / ±15s as sampling noise at this seed count, not a real effect (owner decision,
  E6b).
- It is a yardstick, not a good player. No secret abilities, no Cinghiale, no phone-control quirks modelled
  (a human reacts differently to a close call than a BFS distance field does).
- The avoidance radius (2 tiles) is the same for a lone attrezzo/aereo and for a merged Super Panino, even
  though the Panino is visually bigger — not modelled separately.
- **"Shot hits" undercounts real projectile contact**: a shot that reaches the player while invulnerable
  never calls `killPlayer()` at all, so it's never counted. The number in the tables is "shots that killed",
  not "shots that touched".
- **Map choice for gironi 1–8 (fixed class, random map within it) and 9–12 (random class) is seeded, but
  the "no immediate repeat" rule reads `G.lastMi`, which persists across runs in the same page session** —
  so a run's exact map can be nudged by whichever map the *previous* run in the sweep happened to land on.
  The whole sweep reproduces byte-for-byte on a rerun (same script, same order), but a single cell isn't
  independently reproducible in isolation from the rest of the sequence.
- 1 run out of 240 (`roccia`, girone 10, seed 3, scheduled) hit a pre-existing exception in the shoot/
  projectile path (`Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'NaN')`), unrelated to anything E6a
  touches; the harness catches it, logs it, and moves on rather than losing the run silently. Not
  investigated further here — E6a doesn't touch `index.html`.
- Bonus-% figures for girone 6–8 (roccia) have a wide range because 1–2 of the 5 seeds timed out (girPts
  frozen at whatever was earned before minute 3, no clear-time bonus at all) — the mean blends "cleared with
  a big bonus" and "timed out with none", see the Flags below.

## Flags

1. **The girone-clear bonus (M1b) grows to dominate the girone's own points once several stage-3 ghosts are
   present** — up to 50.7% (algidone girone 7) and 52.4% (girone 8) of the total points earned that girone
   come from the fixed post-hoc multiplier, not from anything the player did during the girone; gironi
   11–12 (all-stage-3 S maps) sit at a flat 42.9% for both characters. Direction: **down** — consider
   trimming `GIR_BONUS.st3` (0.25) or the class multipliers so the bonus stays a top-up rather than
   approaching half the reward.
2. **Girone 6 (the first L-class girone, 5 ghosts, stages [1,1,1,2,2]) has the highest timeout rate in the
   whole sweep** — 2 of 5 seeds for both characters, tied with roccia's girone 8 (2/5) despite girone 6
   having the *lowest* ghost stages of any L-class girone. Direction: **investigate before tuning up** —
   this looks like the S→L class jump itself (4→5 ghosts, bigger map) rather than the stage-2 abilities,
   since girone 6's own ablation (stage 1, still 5 ghosts) clear time is *higher* than the scheduled clear
   time for both characters (111.8→128.6s roccia, 105.5→128.6s algidone) — the ablation run took *longer*
   despite having fewer active abilities, which only makes sense if map/ghost-count variance between seeds
   is a bigger factor here than the abilities are. Numbers, not a fix, are needed first.
3. **Aerei stage-3 shooting fires often but almost never lands** — up to 2.70 shots/min (girone 10) and
   consistently 1.5–2.7/min across gironi 9–12, yet "shot hits" stays at 0.0–0.6 even against a bot with no
   human reaction delay. Sprint, by contrast, contributes to the large majority of ghost-contact deaths at
   the same gironi. Direction: **up** — shoot's projectile speed or the aim telegraph may be too easy to
   read/dodge relative to sprint; CLAUDE.md §10.9 already deferred E2/E3 fine-tuning to E6, and this is
   exactly the kind of number that pass should look at.
4. **Deaths are overwhelmingly direct ghost/aereo contact everywhere, regardless of which abilities are
   active** — projectile and Super Panino death counts stay in the low single digits even at gironi
   10–12 where the pair is very active (up to 8.2 walls broken, 3.0 merges/run for algidone). Direction:
   **check intent** — if stage-2/3 abilities were meant to diversify *how* the player dies or gets pressured
   (not just how fast pellets get eaten or how much score swings), grease/shoot/Super-Panino contact may
   need to matter more directly; if plain contact dominance is fine (abilities are pressure/economy tools,
   not primary killers), no change needed — the data can't tell which was intended.
5. **girPts variance at algidone girone 7 is extreme for only 5 seeds**: mean 4180, range 1500–9900, a
   6.6× spread — the widest in either table. The likely driver is `G.chain` (the ghost-eat chain, up to
   1600 pts per kill, `100*2^chain`): one lucky consecutive-scared-ghost-eating streak can swing a girone's
   total by thousands of points, dwarfing steady pellet income. Direction: **check, don't necessarily
   change** — if big chain swings are the intended "risk/reward" texture of a power-up, leave it; if the
   swings are this wide even for a simple bot that doesn't hunt chains deliberately, a human chaining on
   purpose could swing it further still, worth a look at whether `girPts`/economy is too chain-sensitive at
   busier gironi.
